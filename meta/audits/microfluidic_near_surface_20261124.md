# Near-surface clearance and end-to-end test (2026-11-24)
The previous assessed Z slab 0.15–5.85 mm excludes the first and last 0.15 mm of the base. Four 0.08 mm skins (top/bottom window and top/bottom mounting bore) were rendered locally with OpenSCAD and checked with the exact clearance.py kernel. All four escaped the old slab; all four were rejected by the new slab 0.01–5.99 mm. The correct reference passed both. This is a deliberate predeclared dimensional tolerance, not a claim of exact 0–6 mm clearance. These are kernel-level results, not full upstream scores. The new microfluidic_clearance_e2e.py calls the actual evaluate function with and without the prism check on the same meshes and frozen statistics, reports actual false passes, and asserts acceptance of the reference and rejection of the five defective models. Full CI remains pending. No claim of physical print, real hardware fit, manuscript acceptance or external authorship.
